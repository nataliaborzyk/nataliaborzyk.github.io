FAQ

Good questions

How much does a project cost?

Every quote is prepared individually, because the price depends on the scope, the number of versions and where the design will be used. Write to me about what you need and I will reply with a quote.

How long does it take?

It depends on the project – you will find a typical timeline under each service. I always confirm the deadline in the quote.

How many revisions are included?

The number of revision rounds is agreed in the quote, so you know upfront what is included.

What files will I get?

Print-ready PDFs prepared to your printer’s specification, web formats such as JPG and PNG, and editable source files on request.

Do you issue invoices?

Yes. I run a registered business in Italy (Partita IVA) and invoice companies in Poland, Italy and the rest of the EU.

Do we need to meet in person?

No. I work remotely with clients across Europe – e-mail and video calls are enough, in Polish, English or Italian.
