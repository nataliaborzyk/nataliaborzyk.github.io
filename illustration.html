Hand-drawn surface pattern design

I am a surface pattern designer and illustrator. Every motif is drawn by hand – in Procreate or painted traditionally and scanned – and built into a seamless repeat ready for wallpaper, fabric, stationery, gift wrap and print-on-demand. You can license a pattern from my collection or commission a custom design in your brand’s colours, with up to four colourways. I work with brands and manufacturers worldwide.

What you get

  • A seamless repeat tile in print-ready quality
  • One to four colourways, chosen for where the pattern will be used
  • Files sized for your production: wallpaper, fabric or print-on-demand
  • A licence that fits how you will sell the product
  • Product mockups for your shop

Questions

What is a repeat tile?

A square of the pattern that joins with itself on every side. Your printer repeats it across the whole roll or fabric, so there are no visible edges.

Can I have the pattern in my brand colours?

Yes. Send me your colours and I will prepare a colourway to match them.

Can I sell products with your pattern?

Yes – that is what the licence is for. We agree on the products and the scope before you start selling.
