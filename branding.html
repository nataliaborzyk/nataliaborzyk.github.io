Working in a duo

Two designers, one brand

I take on branding projects together with Roksana Mistal, a graphic designer I trust – we join forces so every identity gets two points of view and twice the care.

  1. We both look for a direction, so you start with more – and more different – concepts.
  2. We choose together what to show you, so only refined proposals reach you.
  3. You get final artwork, a mini brand book and mockups – files ready for print and screen.

What you get

  • Logo concepts and final artwork
  • Vertical, horizontal and single-colour versions
  • Colours, typefaces and a mini brand book with clear rules
  • Mockups of the brand in use: signs, stationery, social media
  • Files for print and screen: PDF, SVG, PNG

Questions

Who will I work with?

With both of us. You write to me, and at the start we agree who will be your contact during the project.

How many logo concepts will I see?

The number is agreed in the quote. Because we work in a duo, you get a wider choice of directions to start with.

Do I need a brand book for a small business?

A short one, yes. Two or three pages are enough for a printer or a sign maker to use your logo correctly.
